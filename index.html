UI control group elements

↔/←/→ Slider animation direction Slider animates automatically in selected direction: ← Left, → Right, or ↔ Ping-pong (back-and-forth). Right-click/long-press for direction options.
⏱️ Slider speed Change speed of slider animation
╭╯Speed scale Change speed growth curve (shape over time; 16+ options) of slider animation (right-click or long-press to mirror, reverse, or flip curve)
🎚️ Slider limits Set slider range (min/max limits); direction & speed scale for slider limit track animation.
🔒۝ Lock/mirror limits Lock and mirror the min and max limits symmetrically relative to the slider's center point
🎲 Random Random slider value within limits (press for 0.5s for continuous 0.1s (default) randomization until pressed again; right-click/long-press for more otions)
↺ Reset Resets a single slider to its default value and stops slider animation
